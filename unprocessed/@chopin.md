# @chopin

1. Best Investments for Beginners: Start by purchasing the Monthly Card as it offers the best value. Next, focus on obtaining Bella and Zendaya, followed by investing in Daily Stream Packs.
2. Teamwork is Key: Always help each other with CD production tasks to ensure the group progresses efficiently.
3. Group Plan Investments: Invest in the Group Plan regularly by navigating to the Group tab, selecting Plan, and following the recommended investment amounts.
4. Building Focus: Prioritise upgrading two main buildings, which are usually the Coffee Shop and the Hair Salon. Avoid over-recruiting employees and only recruit as needed for upgrades. Your money is better spent on Touring rather than unnecessary recruitment.
5. Gathering recommendation get to 30. Only work on 2nd upgrade. Nothing on first. Olivia, Ava. Isabella. Annabella. Mia.
6. Touring for Gold: Touring is the best way to earn gold. Make it a priority to tour frequently and use your earnings to upgrade your two main buildings as soon as possible.
7. Strategic Genre Upgrades: When upgrading girls, prioritise those within the same music genre. Having more girls in the same genre significantly boosts your power due to genre bonuses.
8. Spending Action Points (AP): Complete all your agent gigs, which refresh twice a day and require approximately 500 AP each cycle. When your agent is close to levelling up, save your AP, as you will receive a free refresh upon levelling. Use your remaining AP based on current events. Some events reward stadium rallies, while others reward concert attempts. If there are no active events, prioritise stadium rallies, as they offer the best rewards for you and your group members.
9. Managing Demos and Inspiration Points: Use all your demos initially until you unlock most of your songs. Once the majority of your songs are unlocked, start saving your demos for events, as using them during events grants bonus points. For inspiration points, focus on bringing most of your songs to 3-4 stars, as songs at this level are more likely to become a hot release, providing 2x money and 1.5x fans for idle sales.
10. Logging Off Preparation: Before logging off, ensure your auto rally is activated so you can progress toward your group quest and collect auto rally rewards. Additionally, save at least 150 AP before logging off, as due to an existing bug, this minimum amount is required to join auto rallies successfully. Auto rally only applies to stadium rallies, so your fans are safe from members rallying against other player targets.
11. Upgrading SSRs: Focus on upgrading a few of your SSR girls rather than spreading your resources thinly across many. The main priority should be obtaining duplicate copies of the same girl or at least SSR photos. A low-skill, low-level SSR is far less effective than a few highly skilled and levelled SSRs. SSRs become exponentially more powerful as their skills are upgraded, making it crucial to concentrate your efforts on a select few.
12. Understanding UR Girls: When you obtain a UR girl, its skills and levels are directly linked to one of your SSR girls. Essentially, this means if your SSR girl is highly upgraded, the UR girl becomes just as powerful, effectively doubling its strength. This is another reason why focusing on upgrading a few SSRs over many is so important - your investment in those SSRs will carry over to your UR girls, amplifying their power significantly.
13. Understanding Girl Stats and Equipment: A girl's Singing stat functions like an attack stat, Dancing is like a defence stat, and Manage impacts a girl' s ability to generate money when assigned to buildings. It's advisable to avoid upgrading purple accessories excessively; instead, save your resources for gold accessories such as heels, belts, and makeup, as these provide significantly greater benefits. Players should prioritise upgrading equipment that boosts Singing stats first, followed by a secondary focus on Manage equipment. While AFK, ensure your girls are equipped with Manage accessories to maximise money generation. During war times, switch their accessories to Singing sets for better performance.
14. Understanding Battle: In a rally, the rally leader's girls represent the entire rally, meaning their strength determines the outcome. To maximise the chances of victory and minimise fan losses, only high-ranking members should initiate rallies during war. Similarly, in building defence, it is crucial to have a high-ranking member send their strongest girls to defend, as their girls represent the entire garrison. Proper coordination ensures the best defence and attack strategies for the group.
15. Best Way to Spend Diamonds: The most effective way to spend diamonds is on purchasing properties. For attackers, prioritise acquiring Singing properties to boost their performance in battles. For lower-ranked members, focus on Manage properties to enhance money generation, which supports overall group growth.
16. Building Strategy: The speed of construction depends on the number of unique individuals contributing, not the total fan count. When building without being under threat, each member can simply send a single girl to contribute efficiently. However, when building under threat, all members should still participate to maximise the number of unique contributors. Additionally, R4 and R5 members must ensure that the strongest player available is selected to represent the garrison for defence during the construction.
17. Gold Mining with Spare Vehicles: When you have a spare vehicle, send it to mine gold at the nearest Garden Hotel. For a faster mining speed, send your vehicle to the special Garden Hotels located within our territory, as this significantly reduces mining time (note that the additional gold reward feature is currently bugged). Gold is vital for upgrading buildings, which is the most effective way to quickly increase your financial strength. Prioritise efficient gold mining to support your progress.
18. Landmark Strategy: Holding landmarks provides passive financial strength, with eight levels of landmarks available for possession. Each landmark owned increases financial strength passively. However, holding multiple landmarks of the same level, such as two level-1 landmarks, does not provide additional financial strength. For the benefit of the server, it is better to share such landmarks. That said, it is recommended to first secure the first occupation reward before releasing the landmark to other groups later. This ensures both group progress and server cooperation.
19. Resort Strategy: Resorts only provide a first occupation reward and do not offer passive financial strength when held. Therefore, once the first occupation reward is secured, it is beneficial to let go of resorts and focus on other objectives that contribute to long-term group growth.
20. Skyscraper Strategy: Skyscrapers provide multiple benefits, including a first occupation reward, passive financial strength, and a unique UR girl. There are two levels of Skyscrapers: the first in Zone 1, where we are currently located, and the second in Zone 2. At any given time, we can only hold one Skyscraper per zone, meaning the maximum number of UR girls we can obtain from Skyscrapers is two. Prioritise securing these strategic landmarks to maximise their rewards and benefits.
21. Burj Khalifa Strategy: Controlling the Burj Khalifa grants the chairman the prestigious title of Mayor, allowing them to appoint players across the server to selected seats of influence. This includes the ability to assign both positive and negative titles. Positive titles provide buffs, while negative titles impose nerfs on the individuals holding them. Additionally, the chairman can distribute rewards such as SSR girls and gift boxes to any player in the server. This makes the Burj Khalifa a highly strategic and impactful landmark to control.
22. Bridge Strategy and Map Zones: The map is divided into zones separated by rivers, which can only be crossed if your group owns a bridge. Controlling multiple bridges is crucial for two reasons. First, conquering bridges provides first occupation rewards. Second, owning bridges gives strategic control over which groups can access different zones. As you move closer to the Burj Khalifa, resource levels, such as Garden Hotels and Stadiums, become higher, making it essential to secure bridges to access these valuable zones and maintain an advantage.
