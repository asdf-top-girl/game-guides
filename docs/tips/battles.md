# Battles

In a rally:
- The rally leader's girls represent the entire rally
   - Their strength determines the outcome.
   - To maximise the chances of victory and minimise fan losses, only high-ranking members should initiate rallies during war.
- Same in building defence
- Proper coordination ensures the best defence and attack strategies for the group.
