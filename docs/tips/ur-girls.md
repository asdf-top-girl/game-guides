# UR Girls

The UR girl:
- Its skills and levels are directly linked to one of your SSR girls.
  - This means if your SSR girl is highly upgraded, the UR girl becomes just as powerful, effectively doubling its strength.
  - This is another reason why focusing on upgrading a few SSRs over many is so important - your investment in those SSRs will carry over to your UR girls, amplifying their power significantly.
