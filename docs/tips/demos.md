# Demos

- Use all your demos initially until you unlock most of your songs.
- Once the majority of your songs are unlocked, start saving your demos for events
  - Using them during events grants bonus points.

## Inspiration Points

- Focus on bringing most of your songs to 3-4 stars
  - Songs at this level are more likely to become a hot release (providing 2x money and 1.5x fans for idle sales).
