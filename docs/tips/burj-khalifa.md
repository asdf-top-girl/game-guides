# Burj Khalifa

- Controlling it grants the chairman the prestigious title of Mayor
- The Major can:
  - Appoint players across the server to selected seats of influence.
    - Both positive and negative titles can be assigned.
    - Positive titles provide buffs on the individuals holding them.
    - Negative titles impose nerfs on the individuals holding them.
  - Distribute rewards such as SSR girls and gift boxes to any player in the server.
- This makes it a highly strategic and impactful landmark to control.
