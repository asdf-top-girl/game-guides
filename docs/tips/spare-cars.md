# Spare Cars

- When you have a spare vehicle
  - Send it to mine gold at the nearest Garden Hotel.
    - For a faster mining speed, send your vehicle to the special Garden Hotels located within our territory, as this significantly reduces mining time (note that the additional gold reward feature is currently bugged).
    - Gold is vital for upgrading buildings, which is the most effective way to quickly increase your financial strength.
    - Prioritise efficient gold mining to support your progress.
