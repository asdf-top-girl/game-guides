# Action Points

- Complete all your agent gigs
  - Refresh twice a day.
  - Require approximately 500 AP each cycle.
- When your agent is close to levelling up, save your AP, as you will receive a free refresh upon levelling.
- Use your remaining AP based on current events.
  - Some events reward stadium rallies, while others reward concert attempts.
- If there are no active events, prioritise stadium rallies, as they offer the best rewards for you and your group members.
