# Logging Off

Before logging off
- Ensure your auto-join rally is activated
  - It progresses toward group quest.
  - It collects auto rally rewards.
- Save at least 150 AP before logging off, because due to an existing bug, this minimum amount is required to join auto rallies successfully.
- Auto-join rally only applies to stadium rallies
  - Your fans are safe from members rallying against other player targets.
- Change your girls accessories/assets with Manage accessories to maximise money generation.
  - During war times, switch their accessories to Singing/Dancing sets for better performance.
