# Girls Assets

- Avoid upgrading purple accessories/assets excessively
- Save your Assets Coins for gold accessories/assets.
  - These provide significantly greater benefits.
- Prioritise upgrading equipment that boosts Singing/Dancing stats first.
- Prioritise upgrading by a secondary focus on Manage equipment.
