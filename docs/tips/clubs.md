# Clubs

- The speed of construction depends on the number of unique individuals contributing, not the total fan count.
  - When building without being under threat,
    - Each member can simply send a single girl to contribute efficiently.
  - When building under threat
    - All members should still participate to maximise the number of unique contributors.
    - R4 and R5 members must ensure that the strongest player available is selected to represent the garrison for defence during the construction.
