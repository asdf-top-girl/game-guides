# Girls Upgrade

## Upgrade SSR

- Focus on upgrading a few of your SSR girls rather than spreading your resources thinly across many.
  - The main priority should be obtaining duplicate copies of the same girl or at least SSR photos.
  - A low-skill, low-level SSR is far less effective than a few highly skilled and levelled SSRs.
  - SSRs become exponentially more powerful as their skills are upgraded, making it crucial to concentrate your efforts on a select few.

*Tip From:* **@chopin**

## Style Bonus

Girls on the same Music Genre
- 3 => +2% Sing / Dance
- 4 => +4% Sing / Dance
- 5 => +8% Sing / Dance
